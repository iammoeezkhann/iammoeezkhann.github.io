iWater Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Freeform Technologies

iWater (“the App”) is a personal hydration tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. We built it to help you drink water — not to sell your data.

What we collect

Hydration logs, optional profile details (such as display name, weight used for goal suggestions), reminder preferences, and supporter status are stored on your device. If you enable Apple Health, water intake may be written to Apple Health and optional weight may be read to suggest a goal. Health data stays under Apple’s Health privacy controls.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers or use App Tracking Transparency IDs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your drinking history.

Purchases

Optional one-time in-app purchases (for example, Remove Ads & Support) are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation that you purchased; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, local notifications are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Apple Watch & widgets

The Watch app and widgets use an App Group on your devices to show today’s intake. Data stays on your Apple devices unless you choose Apple services (Health, iCloud if enabled later).

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information for children.

Your choices

  • Revoke Health access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → iWater.
  • Delete the App to remove on-device logs stored only in the App.
