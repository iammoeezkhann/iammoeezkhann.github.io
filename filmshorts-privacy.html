Privacy Policy

App / service: ShortFilms4KQHD (FilmShorts)
Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ShortFilms4KQHD (“we”, “us”, “the app”) handles information when you use our Facebook / Instagram integration and related video publishing tools operated by the app owner.

What this app does

ShortFilms4KQHD is a personal automation tool that publishes short-form videos to social media accounts you control, including Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. The app runs on the owner’s own computer or server.

Information we collect

  • Account tokens: OAuth and Page access tokens needed to post on your behalf (stored locally on the operator’s device).
  • Public profile and Page data: Basic account and Page identifiers returned by Meta, Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok APIs during authorization.
  • Content you provide: Video files, titles, descriptions, and hashtags queued for upload.
  • Upload history: Local records used to avoid duplicate posts.

We do not collect passwords. We do not operate a public user registration system.

How we use information

Information is used only to:

  • Authenticate with Meta (Facebook / Instagram), YouTube, and TikTok APIs
  • Upload and publish content you authorize
  • Prevent duplicate uploads and report upload status

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

Data storage and retention

Credentials, videos, and upload logs are stored locally on the operator’s machine. We do not run a cloud backend that stores end-user data. Tokens may be refreshed or deleted at any time by revoking app access in Meta / TikTok / Google settings or by removing local credential files.

Third-party services

When you connect accounts, your data is processed according to the policies of:

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram)
  • Google / YouTube
  • TikTok

Your rights

You may:

  • Revoke app permissions in your Facebook, Instagram, Google, or TikTok account settings
  • Request deletion of data associated with this app (see below)
  • Contact us with privacy questions at any time

Data deletion instructions

To request deletion of data held by this app, email nordictrends.info@gmail.com with the subject ShortFilms4KQHD — Data Deletion and include:

  • Your Facebook or Instagram username or Page name
  • A brief description of what you want deleted

We will remove locally stored tokens and related records within 30 days and confirm by email. To fully remove Meta’s copy of authorization data, also remove the app from your Facebook account settings → Apps and Websites.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
